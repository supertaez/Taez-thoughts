### 1. **Survival & Future Selves** [citation:1][citation:10]  
> **"You have survived everything you’ve been through... Stay for the person you will become."**  
**Explanation:** Haig reframes survival as a bridge to future possibilities. Your "future self" is a compass for perseverance.  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"When we are no longer able to change a situation, we are challenged to change ourselves."*  
> — Viktor Frankl, *Man’s Search for Meaning*

### 2. **Rock Bottom as Foundation** [citation:1][citation:7]  
> **"The best thing about rock bottom is the rock part... build yourself anew."**  
**Explanation:** Crisis reveals unbreakable core strength. Pain carves space for growth.  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"Rock bottom became the solid foundation on which I rebuilt my life."*  
> — J.K. Rowling, Harvard Speech

### 3. **Anxiety vs. Curiosity**
> **"Curiosity and passion are the enemies of anxiety... via the world."**  
**Explanation:** External engagement disrupts inward spirals.  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"The important thing is not to stop questioning. Curiosity has its own reason for existing."*  
> — Albert Einstein

### 4. **Inherent Self-Worth**
> **"Your worth is your existence... born with worth."**  
**Explanation:** Reject transactional validation. You’re inherently "enough."  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"Worthiness doesn’t have prerequisites."*  
> — Brené Brown, *The Gifts of Imperfection*

### 5. **Direction Over Speed**
> **"Walking one foot in front of the other... further than running in circles."**  
**Explanation:** Consistency trumps frantic motion.  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"The journey of a thousand miles begins with one step."*  
> — Lao Tzu

### 6. **Neutrality of Events**
> **"External events are neutral... mind gives us keys."**  
**Explanation:** Reality is shaped by interpretation.  
**Related Quote:**  
> *"The universe is change; our life is what our thoughts make it."*  
> — Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*
