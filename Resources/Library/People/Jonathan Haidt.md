# Jonathan Haidt: Profile

## Overview
**[[Jonathan Haidt]]** is a renowned social psychologist, professor, and author known for his groundbreaking work on morality, happiness, and societal issues. His research spans diverse fields such as moral psychology, political polarization, and the impact of technology on mental health. Haidt's interdisciplinary approach has made him one of the most influential thinkers of our time.

>  ![](https://cdn.iai.tv/assets/Uploads/_resampled/FillWzQwMCwzNjBd/24-06-07.JH-2.gfw.jpg)  
> _"Morality binds and blinds."_  
> — Jonathan Haidt  

This quote encapsulates his central thesis in *[[The Righteous Mind]]*: human morality serves as both a unifying force within groups and a source of division between them.

---

## Biography

### Early Life and Education
- **Birth:** October 19, 1963, in New York City.
- **Education:** 
  - BA in Philosophy from Yale University (1985).
  - PhD in Psychology from the University of Pennsylvania (1992), where he studied under cultural psychologist Paul Rozin.

### Academic Career
- **Current Position:** Professor of Ethical Leadership at New York University’s Stern School of Business ([NYU Stern](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/faculty/bio/jonathan-haidt)).
- **Previous Roles:** Taught at the University of Virginia for 16 years before joining NYU in 2011.
- **Research Focus:** Morality, ethics, political psychology, and the intersection of technology and mental health.

### Notable Theories and Concepts

#### 1. **Moral Foundations Theory**
- Developed with colleagues, this framework identifies six innate moral foundations that shape human behavior:
  1. Care/Harm
  2. Fairness/Cheating
  3. Loyalty/Betrayal
  4. Authority/Subversion
  5. Sanctity/Degradation
  6. Liberty/Oppression

- **Graphic Representation:**  
![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/Moral_Foundations_Theory.svg/1200px-Moral_Foundations_Theory.svg.png)  
_Source: Wikimedia Commons_

#### 2. **The Rider and the Elephant Metaphor**
- From *The Happiness Hypothesis*, Haidt uses this metaphor to describe the interplay between rational thought ("the rider") and emotional intuition ("the elephant"). This concept has been widely adopted in behavioral economics and psychology.

## Influence and Impact

### Public Intellectual
Haidt is a prominent public intellectual whose ideas have influenced debates on morality, education, and technology. He frequently contributes to media outlets like *The Atlantic*, *The Wall Street Journal*, and TED Talks.

- **TED Talk:** ["The Moral Roots of Liberals and Conservatives"](https://www.ted.com/talks/jonathan_haidt_the_moral_roots_of_liberals_and_conservatives)  
Explains how liberals and conservatives prioritize different moral foundations.

### Advocacy for Mental Health Reform
Through *[[The Anxious Generation]]*, Haidt advocates for systemic changes to address the mental health crisis among adolescents, including regulating social media and promoting offline activities.

---

## Controversies and Criticisms

While Haidt’s work is widely respected, it has also sparked debate:

1. **Political Polarization:** Some critics argue that his emphasis on group morality oversimplifies complex political dynamics.
2. **Technology Critique:** Tech companies and proponents of digital innovation sometimes dismiss his warnings about screen time and social media as alarmist.

Despite these criticisms, Haidt remains committed to evidence-based solutions and open dialogue.
