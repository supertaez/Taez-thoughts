## 1. Control the Controllable (Stoic Dichotomy)

> “We suffer more in imagination than in reality.” – Seneca

- **Core Idea:** Separate what’s in your power (actions, judgments) from what’s not (others’ actions, outcomes).  
- **Underestimated Impact:** Most stress, anger, and wasted energy stem from chasing externals.  
- **Actionable:**  
  - Daily journaling: list today’s “externals” vs. “internals.”  
  - Premeditatio malorum: visualize potential setbacks to reduce shock.  

> **See also:** [[Stoicism & Resilience]] 1

## 2. Reality Is Filtered (Cognitive Biases)

> **Callout:**  
> “Your brain edits raw data into a story that feels real.”  

| Bias               | Effect                                       |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Confirmation Bias  | Seeks info that confirms your views          |
| Availability Heur. | Overweights vivid or recent examples         |
| Loss Aversion      | Fears losses twice as much as valuing gains  |

- **Why It Shifts Everything:** Recognizing biases dismantles illusions—your “truth” is often your brain’s best guess.  
- **Exercise:**  
  1. Pick a decision.  
  2. List 3 ways you might be biased.  
  3. Seek one disconfirming data point.  

> **See also:** [[Cognitive Biases Catalog]] 2

## 3. You Are the Storyteller (Narrative Identity)

> “We live stories, not facts.” – Dan McAdams

- **Key Insight:** Your self‑image is a narrative you continually edit.  
- **Why You Miss It:** You assume your past “just happened,” ignoring how you frame events.  
- **Practice:** Rewrite a “limiting” life event with a growth lens.  

> **See also:** [[Narrative Identity]]

## 4. Mindsets Matter More Than Talent (Growth vs. Fixed)

> **Callout:**  
> “Believing abilities can improve makes you actually improve.”  

- **Fixed Mindset:** Talent is innate → Avoid challenges, fear failure.  
- **Growth Mindset:** Effort builds skill → Embrace challenges, learn from feedback.  
- **Under the Hood:** Mindset shapes neural pathways—what you believe changes how you learn.  

> **See also:** [[Growth Mindset Primer]] 3

## 5. Feedback Is Your Fuel (Iterative Learning)

| Stage         | Action                                 |
|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| Plan          | Hypothesize & set a small test        |
| Do            | Execute experiment                    |
| Check         | Gather data & honest feedback         |
| Act           | Adjust and repeat                     |

- **Why It Matters:** Real‑world progress isn’t linear; it’s a spiral of learning.  
- **Tip:** Solicit “red‑teaming” feedback from someone who disagrees with you.  

> **See also:** [[Feedback Loops]]

## 6. Systems > Goals (Design Your Operating System)

> “You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.” – James Clear

- **System:** The processes and habits that produce results.  
- **Common Mistake:** Obsessing over outcomes (weight, promotion) without building the daily rituals.  
- **Quick Win:** Map your morning routine as a flowchart—identify one friction point to eliminate.  

> **See also:** [[Systems Thinking Toolkit]] 4

## 7. Meaning Over Pleasure (Eudaimonia)

- **Philosophy:** Aristotle’s concept of flourishing—pursuing virtue and purpose, not just hedonic pleasure.  
- **Underestimated:** Momentary happiness doesn’t build a life; meaningful challenges do.  
- **Action:** Define your “north star”—the contribution you want to be known for.  

> **See also:** [[Purpose & Meaning]]

---

## 8. Experience Trumps Theory (Learning by Doing)

> “Knowledge without practice is meaningless.” – John Dewey

- **Experiential Learning Cycle:** Concrete experience → Reflection → Conceptualization → Experimentation.  
- **Why You Underuse It:** Comfort with theory; fear of messy real‑world feedback.  
- **Next Step:** Prototype a “micro‑project” today—apply any idea, reflect, iterate.  

> **See also:** [[Experiential Learning]] 5

---

## 9. Self-Awareness Is the Foundation (Meta‑Cognition)

- **Definition:** Thinking about your thinking—observing emotions, biases, triggers.  
- **Why It’s Overlooked:** Busy minds default to autopilot.  
- **Practice:** 5‑minute morning check‑in—note your emotional “baseline” before work.  

> **See also:** [[Self-Awareness Practices]]

---

## 10. We Rise by Lifting Others (Altruism & Compassion)

- **Psychology:** Helping others activates your brain’s reward centers.  
- **Philosophy:** Kant’s categorical imperative—act so your maxims can be universal laws.  
- **Action:** Schedule one “service” activity weekly: mentor, volunteer, or random act of kindness.  

> **See also:** [[Altruism & Compassion]] 6
