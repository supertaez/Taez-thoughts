# 📅 KG1 Year Plan (200 Learning Days + 50 Fun Days)

>[!info] About This Plan
>This comprehensive year plan for KG1 is designed with a play-based approach to learning, covering essential developmental areas through engaging activities. Each week consists of 4 learning days followed by 1 fun day that reinforces the week's concepts through games and interactive activities.

## 📚 How To Use This Plan

This curriculum is organized into 20 thematic clusters, with each cluster containing 10 days of learning. Within each week, the pattern follows:
- 4 days of structured learning activities
- 1 day of fun, games, and reinforcement activities

>[!tip] Navigation Tip
>All activity titles are linked to their detailed lesson plans. Click on any `[[linked title]]` to access the full lesson content.

## 🎨 Cluster 1: Letters & Sounds (Weeks 1-2)

### Week 1
1. Day 1: [[Alphabet Adventure]] - Introduction to the alphabet through a classroom treasure hunt where children discover letters hidden around the learning environment. Each letter found prompts a discussion about its sound and words that begin with it.
2. Day 2: [[Sound Safari]] - Children go on an auditory adventure, listening for and identifying different beginning sounds in words. They create sound boxes with objects that start with target phonemes.
3. Day 3: [[Rhyming Rainforest]] - Exploration of rhyming words through stories and games in a rainforest-themed classroom. Children create rhyming pairs using picture cards and body movements.
4. Day 4: [[Letter Treasure Hunt]] - Focus on letter recognition through a pirate-themed treasure hunt. Children follow clues to find "treasure" (letters) and match uppercase to lowercase.
5. Day 5: **FUN DAY** - [[Alphabet Games Day]] - Various stations with alphabet games including alphabet hopscotch, letter fishing, alphabet bingo, and a letter dance party.

### Week 2
6. Day 6: [[Vowel Voyage]] - Introduction to vowels through songs, stories, and movement. Children learn about the special role vowels play in words.
7. Day 7: [[Consonant Carnival]] - Exploration of consonants through hands-on activities and games. Children create consonant crafts and participate in sound-matching games.
8. Day 8: [[Magical Phonemes]] - Children discover how phonemes (individual sounds) combine to create words through interactive play and manipulatives.
9. Day 9: [[Word Builders Workshop]] - Using letter blocks and magnetic letters, children experiment with building simple CVC (consonant-vowel-consonant) words.
10. Day 10: **FUN DAY** - [[Letter Olympics]] - Physical games involving letter recognition, phonemic awareness, and word building challenges in an Olympic-themed event.

>[!note] Connection to Literacy Development
>These foundational activities in Cluster 1 establish early literacy skills that will be built upon throughout the year. The focus on phonological awareness is critical for later reading success.

## 🔢 Cluster 2: Numbers & Counting (Weeks 3-4)

### Week 3
11. Day 11: [[Number Quest]] - Introduction to numbers 1-5 through a quest where children search for number symbols and corresponding quantities around the classroom.
12. Day 12: [[Counting Critters]] - Children practice one-to-one correspondence by counting various toy animals and insects, matching numerals to quantities.
13. Day 13: [[Ten Frame Fun]] - Introduction to ten frames as a visual tool for understanding number relationships. Children place counters in ten frames to represent numbers.
14. Day 14: [[Number Line Jump]] - Physical activity where children hop along a giant number line on the floor, developing understanding of number sequence and relative position.
15. Day 15: **FUN DAY** - [[Counting Carnival]] - Stations featuring counting games with prizes, number recognition activities, and quantity-matching challenges.

### Week 4
16. Day 16: [[Counting in the Clouds]] - Children create cloud-shaped number cards and practice counting objects that correspond to each number, focusing on numbers 6-10.
17. Day 17: [[Math Mountain Climb]] - Visual representation of number bonds using a mountain metaphor. Children explore how numbers can be broken down into parts.
18. Day 18: [[Subitizing Safari]] - Development of subitizing skills (instantly recognizing small quantities without counting) through quick-show games with dot patterns and objects.
19. Day 19: [[Shape of Numbers]] - Exploration of number formation through tactile activities. Children form numbers using playdough, sand writing, and body positions.
20. Day 20: **FUN DAY** - [[Number Olympics]] - Active games involving number recognition, counting challenges, and quantity comparisons in an Olympic-themed event.

>[!quote] Mathematical Foundation
>"Mathematics is not about numbers, equations, computations, or algorithms: it is about understanding." - William Paul Thurston
