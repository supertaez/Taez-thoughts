# 🧠 Lesson Overview

This 3-hour lesson introduces KG1 students to the basics of coding through engaging, age-appropriate activities. Utilizing a blend of unplugged exercises, interactive videos, and printable resources, students will develop foundational skills in sequencing, pattern recognition, and problem-solving.5

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- 583-2Understand that coding is a way to give instructions to computers or robots. 
- 583-3Follow and create simple sequences of instructions. 
- 583-4Recognize and use directional terms (e.g., up, down, left, right). 
- 583-5Collaborate with peers to solve basic problems using step-by-step instructions. 18

## ⏰ Lesson Breakdown

### 🕒 Hour 1: Introduction to Coding Concepts

**Activity 1: What is Coding?**

- **Video:** 1192-4[What is Coding? | Coding & Computer Science Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpeyoRSpMNg) 
- **Discussion:** 1192-6Talk about how coding is like giving instructions to a friend or a robot. 41

**Activity 2: Sequencing with Daily Routines**

- **Exercise:** 1507-2Have students sequence their morning routines using picture cards. 
- **Objective:** 1507-4Understand the importance of order in instructions. 48

**Activity 3: Directional Movement Game**

- **Setup:** 1713-2Place directional mats on the floor. 
- **Exercise:** 1713-4Students follow verbal instructions to move in specific directions. 
- **Objective:** 1713-6Reinforce understanding of directional terms. 58

---

### 🕒 Hour 2: Unplugged Coding Activities

**Activity 4: Coding the Path**

- **Resource:** 1961-3[Free Printable Coding Activity: Rabbit to Carrot](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/free-printable-coding-activity-rabbit-to-carrot--929360073089036526/) 
- **Exercise:** 1961-5Students use arrow cards to guide the rabbit to the carrot on a grid. 
- **Objective:** 1961-7Apply sequencing and directional skills. 68

**Activity 5: Human Robot Game**

- **Setup:** 2361-2Pair students; one is the "robot," and the other is the "programmer." 
- **Exercise:** 2361-4The programmer gives step-by-step instructions to the robot to navigate a simple obstacle course. 
- **Objective:** 2361-6Practice giving and following precise instructions. 78

### 🕒 Hour 3: Creative Coding and Assessment

**Activity 6: Story Sequencing**

- **Exercise:** 2669-3Students create simple stories by sequencing images and narrating the sequence. 
- **Objective:** 2669-5Enhance understanding of order and storytelling. 85

**Activity 7: Coding Worksheet Completion**

- **Resource:** 2922-2[Kodable Kindergarten Worksheets](https://www.kodable.com/resources/k-worksheets) 
- **Exercise:** 2922-4Students complete worksheets that involve coloring and sequencing tasks. 
- **Objective:** 2922-6Assess understanding of coding concepts in a fun way. 95

**Activity 8: Reflection and Sharing**

- **Discussion:** 3233-2Students share what they learned and enjoyed about the activities. 
- **Objective:** 3233-4Reinforce learning through verbal expression. 102
