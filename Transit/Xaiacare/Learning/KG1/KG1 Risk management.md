# 🧠 Lesson Overview

This lesson introduces KG1 students to basic risk management concepts through interactive activities, storytelling, and multimedia resources. The aim is to help young learners recognize safe and unsafe situations, understand the importance of rules, and develop decision-making skills.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Identify common safe and unsafe scenarios in their environment.
- Understand the role of rules in ensuring safety.
- Demonstrate the ability to make safe choices during play and daily activities.
- Recognize and express feelings related to safety and risk.

## 🗂️ Lesson Structure

| Time | Activity | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 0:00 - 0:15 | Welcome & Warm-up | Greeting students and introducing the topic through a simple song or rhyme about safety. |
| 0:15 - 0:45 | Story Time | Reading a story that highlights safety and risk, followed by a discussion. |
| 0:45 - 1:15 | Interactive Video | Watching an age-appropriate video on safety, with guided questions. |
| 1:15 - 1:45 | Role-Playing | Engaging students in role-play scenarios to practice safe decision-making. |
| 1:45 - 2:15 | Creative Activity | Drawing or crafting to reinforce safety concepts learned. |
| 2:15 - 2:45 | Game Time | Playing a game that involves identifying safe and unsafe actions. |
| 2:45 - 3:00 | Recap & Assessment | Reviewing key points and assessing understanding through a simple quiz or discussion. |

## 📚 Materials Needed

- Storybook: *"Officer Buckle and Gloria"* by Peggy Rathmann
- Video: [Safety Rules for Kids](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5aX9A7k4Y8)
- Art supplies: crayons, paper, scissors, glue
- Props for role-playing: toy traffic signs, helmets, etc.
- Game materials: flashcards with images depicting safe and unsafe behaviors

## 📖 Story Time

**Book:** *Officer Buckle and Gloria* by Peggy Rathmann

**Discussion Questions:**

- What safety tips did Officer Buckle share?
- How did Gloria help teach safety?
- Can you think of a safety rule you follow at home or school?

## 🎬 Interactive Video

**Video:** [Safety Rules for Kids](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5aX9A7k4Y8)

**Guided Questions:**

- What are some of the safety rules mentioned?
- Why is it important to follow these rules?
- Can you share a time when you followed a safety rule?

## 🎭 Role-Playing Scenarios

- Crossing the street safely
- Wearing a helmet while biking
- Not talking to strangers
- Asking for help when needed

**Instructions:**

- Divide students into small groups.
- Assign each group a scenario.
- Allow time for preparation and then have each group perform their role-play.

## 🎨 Creative Activity

**Task:** Draw a picture of yourself following a safety rule.

**Instructions:**

- Provide art supplies to each student.
- Encourage them to think of a safety rule they follow and illustrate it.
- Display the drawings in the classroom.

## 🎲 Game Time

**Game:** "Safe or Unsafe?"

**Instructions:**

- Show students flashcards with different scenarios.
- Ask them to decide if the action is safe or unsafe.
- Discuss each scenario and explain the reasoning.

## 📝 Assessment

- Observe student participation during activities.
- Ask students to share one safety rule they learned.
- Provide positive feedback and reinforce correct understanding.
