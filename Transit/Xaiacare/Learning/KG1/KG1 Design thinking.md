# KG1 Design Thinking Lesson Plan: "Creative Problem-Solvers in Training" 

> [!tip] Pedagogy Note  
> This lesson simplifies the **5-stage design thinking process** (Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test) into **3 age-appropriate phases**: "Feel, Imagine, Make" .

---

## Lesson Objectives
1. **Empathy Development**: Identify feelings/needs of others through storytelling ([NGSS K-ESS3-3]).  
2. **Creative Ideation**: Generate 2-3 solutions to a simple problem using tactile materials .  
3. **Collaborative Making**: Build a low-fidelity prototype with recycled materials (CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.SL.K.1) .

## 3-Hour Lesson Breakdown

### Phase 1: **Feel** (Empathize & Define) [45 mins]
1. **Empathy Circle** (20 mins):  
   - Watch: [Sesame Street: What is Empathy?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_1Rt1R4xbM)  
   - Activity: Use printable emotion cards to role-play scenarios (e.g., "How would you feel if your toy broke?") .  
   - Assessment: Teacher observes verbal/non-verbal empathy responses.  

2. **Problem Storytime** (25 mins):  
   - Read: *"The Little Red Hen"* (adaptable to teamwork themes)  
   - Guide students to identify the hen's problem: "No one helps her bake bread!"  
   - Printable Worksheet: Draw faces showing how characters feel .  

![Empathy Example](https://images.twinkl.co.uk/tw1n/image/private/t_630_eco/photo/msid-921735892)

---

### Phase 2: **Imagine** (Ideate) [60 mins]
1. **Brainstorm Bonanza** (30 mins):  
   - Use "Yes, And..." improv rules: Build on peers' ideas .  
   - Prompt: "How can we help the Little Red Hen?"  
   - Document ideas on a collaborative "Idea Web" poster.  

2. **Material Exploration** (30 mins):  
   - Stations with recycled materials (egg cartons, straws), craft supplies, and nature items.  
   - Guided Questions: "Which material is strongest? Softest? Best for holding bread?"  

> [!warning] Safety Note  
> Avoid small choking hazards. Pre-cut materials for fine motor support .

---

### Phase 3: **Make** (Prototype & Test) [75 mins]
1. **Jellybean Engineering** (45 mins):  
   - Challenge: Build a "bread basket" using jellybeans and toothpicks .  
   - Video Demo: [How to Build Jellybean Structures](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ZldQdcSR5Y)  
   - Success Criteria: Holds 3 playdough "bread rolls".  

2. **Gallery Walk** (30 mins):  
   - Students present prototypes using sentence stems: "I made... It helps by..."  
   - Peer Feedback: Drop pom-poms into labeled jars ("I like...", "I wonder...") .
