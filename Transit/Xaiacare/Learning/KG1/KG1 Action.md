# **3-Hour Play-Based Physics Lesson: Understanding Action Through Movement**

## Overview

This lesson introduces kindergarten students (KG1) to the concept of _action_ in physics by using movement, play, simple cause-and-effect observations, and engaging videos. The abstract idea is simplified to relate to everyday experiences like running, jumping, and rolling objects.

---

## **Lesson Objectives**

By the end of the session, learners should be able to:

- Recognize that movement involves effort and time ("action")
- Identify different kinds of motion: fast, slow, rolling, bouncing
- Predict and compare how objects move on different surfaces
- Use play to explore "easy" and "hard" paths (building intuition for least action)
- Verbally describe what they see ("The ball goes faster here!")

---

## **Lesson Breakdown**

### **Part 1: Let’s Move! (30 mins)**

**Objective:** Children experience physical motion and compare effort.

**Activities:**

- Movement game: _"Fast, Slow, Go, Stop!"_
- Crawl vs walk vs run from one side of the room to the other
- Obstacle course: compare a straight path vs twisty one

> [!TIP] Ask guiding questions: "Which was easier? Which was faster? Which made you tired?"

### **Part 2: Watch and Wonder (30 mins)**

**Objective:** Observe movement in the world.

**Resources:**

- [SciShow Kids - How Do Things Move?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSyUb0dJz2I)
- [Peekaboo Kidz - What is Motion?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HtvpJ2J2U0)

**Activity:**

- Watch videos in short segments
- Pause for group discussion: "What moved? How did it move? What made it go?"

> [!QUESTION] Why did the ball roll faster on the smooth floor?

---

### **Part 3: Let’s Explore! (1 hour)**

**Objective:** Investigate how action changes with surface and slope.

**Materials:**

- Toy cars or balls
- Ramps (wooden planks, cardboard)
- Surfaces (towels, foil, sandpaper, floor mat)

**Activities:**

1. **Ramp Races**: Let cars go down ramps of different steepness.
2. **Smooth vs Rough**: Test rolling on soft vs hard surfaces.
3. **Make Your Own Path**: Use blocks to build straight and curvy tracks.

**Guided language:** "Which path helped the car move the most easily?"

![Rolling Cars on Ramp](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/59/Toy_cars_on_a_ramp_experiment.jpg)

---

### **Part 4: Story Time and Reflection (30 mins)**

**Objective:** Connect movement to storytelling.

**Storybook:**

- ["Motion: Push and Pull, Fast and Slow" by Darlene R. Stille](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1445366.Motion)

**Activities:**

- Group story reading
- Children draw or color their favorite way to move
- Share: "I made a bumpy road! It was hard for the car."

> [!NOTE] Use a picture wall or chart paper to record children's descriptions of movement.

---

## **Assessment (30 mins)**

**Objective:** Observe understanding through play and talk.

**Assessment Methods:**

- Verbal responses during play and video discussions
- Observation checklist:
    - Can describe easy vs hard movement?
    - Can compare smooth and rough paths?
    - Participated in building and testing tracks?
- Artwork and storytelling reflection
