# 3-Hour Lesson Plan: Introduction to Algorithms for KG1

![Algorithm Concept for Kids](https://images.twinkl.co.uk/tw1n/image/private/t_630/image_repo/1a/8b/au-t-1665655718-eyfs-early-algorithms-ordering-and-sequencing-activity-sheet_ver_1.jpg)

## **Lesson Overview**  
This lesson introduces Kindergarteners (KG1) to the concept of algorithms through hands-on, unplugged activities, storytelling, and collaborative play. The focus is on **sequencing**, **logical reasoning**, and **debugging** (fixing mistakes). Activities align with CSTA K-12 standards for early computational thinking .

---

## **Learning Objectives**  
1. **Define** an algorithm as a sequence of steps to solve a problem .  
2. **Create** simple algorithms for everyday tasks (e.g., building a Lego tower, planting a seed).  
3. **Debug** incorrect sequences by identifying and fixing errors .  
4. **Connect** algorithms to real-life routines (e.g., brushing teeth, making a sandwich) .

### **Activity Details**  

#### **1. Lego Building Algorithm**   
- **Task**: Students build a simple Lego model, then decompose it step-by-step to create instructions for peers.  
- **Example**:  
  > "First, place a red block. Then, add a blue block on top."  
- **Debugging**: If the peer’s model doesn’t match, students identify missing steps.  

#### **2. Plant a Seed Algorithm**   
- Students cut and paste steps from the worksheet (e.g., "Add soil," "Place seed," "Water lightly").  
- **Cross-Curricular Link**: Connect to science by discussing plant growth.  

#### **3. Fix the Broken Routine**   
- Use Twinkl’s sequencing cards to rearrange steps for tasks like brushing teeth. Ask:  
  > "What happens if we put toothpaste *after* brushing?"  

---

## **Assessment Strategies**  
1. **Observational Checklist**: Track participation in debugging activities.  
2. **Algorithm Drawings**: Students draw a 3-step routine (e.g., washing hands) using arrows.  
3. **Peer Review**: Partners evaluate each other’s Lego algorithms for clarity.  

| Criteria                  | Emerging | Developing | Proficient         |
|---------------------------|----------|------------|--------------------|
| Sequences steps logically | 🟡        | 🟠          | 🟢 (No errors)     |
| Uses clear language       | 🟡        | 🟠          | 🟢 ("First, then") | 

---

## **Extensions for Advanced Learners**  
- **Challenge**: Create a 6-step algorithm for making a sandwich .  
- **Tech Integration**: Use [Lightbot](https://lightbot.com/) (simple coding game) for sequencing practice .  

---

## **Key Takeaways**  
```callout
Algorithms are like recipes! They need clear steps in the right order. If something goes wrong, we can "debug" it by checking each step.
```
