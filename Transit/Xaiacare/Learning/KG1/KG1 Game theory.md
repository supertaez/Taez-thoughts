# 🌟 Game Theory Foundations for Young Learners

![[Colorful Cooperation Image]](https://images.pexels.com/photos/145685/pexels-photo-145685.jpeg)

> *"We can make everyone smile when we work together!"* 

---

## 🎯 Lesson Objectives
1. **Basic Cooperation**: Understand sharing as foundation of game theory's "win-win" concept 
2. **Simple Decision Making**: Choose between "mine" and "ours" in play scenarios
3. **Pattern Recognition**: Identify happy/sad outcomes in social interactions
4. **Emotional Literacy**: Connect feelings to shared outcomes ("We both smiled!")

---

## 📚 Lesson Structure

### 🕒 **Block 1: Warm-Up with "The Sharing Song" (0:00-0:30)**

![[Sharing Song Video]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KfHw8H8u6Is)
**Activity**: Dance freeze game with sharing prompts  
**Resources**: 
- [Printable Emotion Cards](https://www.twinkl.com/resource/t-s-2548898-sharing-social-situation)
- [Collaborative Coloring Sheet](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/9d/25/7e/9d257e5c98b4b2aeb41705b5d0206e48.jpg)

**Teacher Script**:  
*"When the music stops, find a friend and say one thing you can share! Look at these faces – which shows our sharing smile?"*

---

### 🕒 **Block 2: Story Time & Puppet Show (0:30-1:00)** 
**Interactive Video**: [*"The Bear Who Shared"* Animated Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6N5JlIq7_hk)  
**Follow-Up Activity**: 
1. Felt board storytelling with animal puppets
2. "What should the bunny do?" decision points

**Assessment Check**:  
✅ Can identify "everyone happy" vs "someone sad" outcomes  
✅ Uses vocabulary: "share", "take turns", "together"

---

### 🕒 **Block 3: Play-Based Learning Stations (1:00-2:00)** 

| Station       | Game Theory Concept       | Materials Needed          |
|---------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Block Tower   | Collaborative Success     | Mega Bloks® + timer       |
| Snack Math    | Resource Distribution     | Goldfish crackers & cups  | 
| Emotion Pond  | Outcome Recognition       | Lily pad cutouts + emojis |

**Station Rotation Schedule**:
1. 0-15min: Block Tower Challenge
2. 15-30min: Snack Division Game
3. 30-45min: Emotion Pond Jump
4. 45-60min: Free Play Observation

---

### 🕒 **Block 4: Digital Interaction (2:00-2:30)** 
**Online Game**: [PBS Kids - "Share it Fair"](https://pbskids.org/games/sharing/)  
**Interactive Whiteboard Activity**:  
![[Cooperative Puzzle]](https://www.mathsisfun.com/puzzles/images/share-the-sweets.svg)

**Tech Integration Tips**:  
> 💡 Use tablet mirrors to show real-time sharing  
> 💡 Record children explaining their "sharing strategies"

---

### 🕒 **Block 5: Outdoor Application (2:30-3:00)** 
**Playground Games with Theory Twists**:
1. **Modified Duck-Duck-Goose**: "Share-Shake-Skip" version
2. **Nature Scavenger Hunt**: Collaborative list completion
3. **Bubble Cooperation**: Shared bubble wand challenges

**Assessment Rubric**:  
![[Outdoor Rubric Chart]](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/product/Cooperation-Rubric-1832838)

## 🔍 Formative Assessments

   - Uses "we" language during play
   - Points to happy faces when discussing group success
