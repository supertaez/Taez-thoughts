## Summary

Phonics instruction is best when it is explicit, systematic, and engaging, helping KG1 learners connect letters to sounds through multi-sensory activities and meaningful practice ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/reading-101/reading-101-learning-modules/course-modules/phonics?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). This three‑hour lesson weaves together phonological awareness, letter‑sound correspondence, blending, and segmenting, guided by the Science of Reading framework to support neural pathways for reading fluency ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/topics/phonics-and-decoding/articles/phonics-instruction?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). Multimedia resources like ChuChu TV’s “Phonics Song with Two Words” capture attention and reinforce learning through song and movement ([youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n4enj6YMnw&utm_source=chatgpt.com), [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChuChu_TV?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

Learners will draw connections between phonemic skills and emergent literacy, setting the foundation for reading readiness. Links to supporting research and related Obsidian notes ([[Phonics-Foundations]], [[Emergent-Literacy]], [[Science-of-Reading]]) encourage deeper exploration and cross‑referencing.

## Objectives

- **Identify** and **name** all uppercase and lowercase letters and their corresponding sounds with 90% accuracy in flashcard drills ([readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/reading-101/reading-101-learning-modules/course-modules/phonics/practice?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
- **Blend** and **segment** CVC (consonant‑vowel‑consonant) words during guided practice, demonstrating decoding skills ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
- **Manipulate** phonemes through rhyming, syllable counting, and onset‑rime activities to build phonemic awareness ([readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home/reading-101-guide-parents/your-kindergartener/phonological-and-phonemic-awareness?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
- **Engage** with interactive songs and videos, sustaining attention and participation throughout the 3‑hour lesson.

## Activities Explained

> [!note] **Multi‑Sensory Approach:** Combining visual, auditory, and kinesthetic elements supports diverse learners ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

1. **Alphabet Review:** Quick, high‑energy flashcard routine to build automaticity ([readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/reading-101/reading-101-learning-modules/course-modules/phonics/practice?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
2. **Phonological Awareness:** Rhyming and syllable tasks strengthen phonemic skills, crucial before letter‑sound instruction ([readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home/reading-101-guide-parents/your-kindergartener/phonological-and-phonemic-awareness?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
3. **Letter‑Sound Instruction:** Explicit teaching of letters and sounds, following the 7‑step model for phonics lessons ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
4. **Blending & Segmenting:** Guided practice using manipulatives helps bridge phonemic awareness to decoding ([readingrockets.org](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home/reading-101-guide-parents/your-kindergartener/phonics-and-decoding-activities?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
5. **Interactive Games:** Fun consolidation to maintain engagement and practice skills in context.
6. **Assessment:** Exit tickets ensure comprehension and inform next steps.

## Assessments

> [!tip] Use exit tickets and thumbs‑up/thumbs‑down quick checks throughout to monitor progress ([luckylittlelearners.com](https://luckylittlelearners.com/how-to-teach-an-explicit-phonics-lesson/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

- **Formative:** Flashcard accuracy, participation in blending games.
- **Summative:** Exit tickets scoring (pass = ≥ 75% correct).
- **Observational:** Note‑taking during student responses for individual support.
