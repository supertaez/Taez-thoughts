## Infancy (0–12 Months)  
### Social-Emotional Milestones  
- **0–6 Months**: Smiles at caregivers, minimal stranger anxiety.  
- **6–12 Months**: Peak [[Stranger Anxiety]]; separation protests ([[Separation Anxiety]]).  
- **12+ Months**: Uses [[Social Referencing]] to navigate unfamiliar situations.  

### Cognitive Milestones  
- **Object Permanence** (Piaget, 1954): Understanding objects exist when out of sight.  
- **Joint Attention**: Shared focus with caregivers (Tomasello, 1995).  

---

## Toddlerhood (1–3 Years)  
- **Language Development**: Labels emotions (e.g., "scared" of strangers).  
- **Theory of Mind**: Begins understanding others’ perspectives (Wellman, 1990).  

---

## Preschool (3–5 Years)  
- **Social Play**: Cooperative interactions reduce stranger wariness.  
- **Emotional Regulation**: Uses strategies like self-soothing ([[Emotional Regulation Techniques]]).  

> [!WARNING]- Red Flags  
> Persistent stranger anxiety beyond age 3 may warrant evaluation for [[Social Anxiety Disorder]].  

---

## Influencing Factors  
1. **Genetics**: Heritability estimates range from 30–50% (Plomin et al., 2016).  
2. **Environment**: High-stress households exacerbate anxiety ([[Family Stress Model]]).
