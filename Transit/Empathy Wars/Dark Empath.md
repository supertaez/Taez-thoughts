A **dark empath** is a term used to describe an individual who possesses high levels of cognitive empathy (the ability to understand and interpret others' emotions) but uses this ability for manipulative or self-serving purposes. Unlike traditional empaths, who use their empathy to connect with and help others, dark empaths may exploit their understanding of others' emotions to control, deceive, or harm them.

## Key Characteristics
- **High cognitive empathy**: Ability to accurately read and understand others' emotions.
- **Low emotional empathy**: Limited ability to feel or share others' emotions.
- **Manipulative tendencies**: Uses empathy to influence or control others.
- **Self-serving behavior**: Prioritizes personal gain over others' well-being.
- **Dark triad traits**: May exhibit traits of narcissism, Machiavellianism, or psychopathy.
