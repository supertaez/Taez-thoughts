The garbage truck crashed into our car.

My wife called right away to let me know she was okay, but she needed insurance information that wasn't in the glove box. Too many minutes passed. I started stressing. At last, I found and sent her the insurance documents she needed via iMessage. And I realized it was time to get my digital clutter under control.

Ever wished you were more organized?

You may have been in similar trenches: thousands of notes in Apple Notes scattered all over. Some are tagged, others not. Some are in the right folder, but most are "somewhere."

Apple Notes' search function is fast and powerful. But it's useless if you forgot _what_ you archived. Have you ever "hoarded" links, recipes, or snippets? And then you can't remember _where_ they are?

Perhaps your entire Notes domain is such a mess that you've stopped using it altogether. Your notes are chaos, filled with web links, screenshots, lists, meeting minutes, or project notes. Sound familiar?

There is a solution that can come to your rescue, a workflow that is minimalistic and elegant. It can scale and adapt to your needs.

#### Apple Notes, powered by your own arc reactor

Notes got a nice upgrade with all the new bells and whistles from iOS 18 and macOS Sequoia: highlighting, calculations, collapsible sections, and your personal English professor in the form of Apple Intelligence Writing Tools. But it can still be a bit of the wild west, disordered and messy.

To combat the chaos, Matthias Hilse has brought a turbo jetpack and lasers for your Notes app, and even a popcorn maker. _**For free**_.

**Ready to go from ordinary citizen to superhero?**

> "Forever Notes is not an app, but a simple framework and scalable digital note-taking method for Apple Notes. It's free and created to last — forever."

> — Matthias Hilse

**Forever Notes** tips the hat to other organizational systems. It leverages Apple Notes in a fresh way. It helps you organize projects and information. You track the different roles you have. And it integrates journaling.

I've spent years in the jungle of digital organization, as a former store manager for an Apple Premium Reseller and an Evernote Business Certified Consultant. I've tried most tools and systems, but I'm not always as organized as I'd like. **Forever Notes** combines the best of organizational methods into a simple, elegant framework that works.

Thanks to a tiered approach, tags, and linked notes, we get a framework that can grow with us. From the website listed below, Apple shortcuts make pieces of the system assemble themselves.

#### The five core elements of Forever Notes

1. **Home** is a single note with links to the other parts. Think of it as a dashboard. It is the central hub of this note-taking system. You get a "one-stop shop" for the rest of the hierarchy. This is a repository for frequently used resources, links, and information.

All other notes link back to this mothership. It is the base for the framework and methodology that will turn even the most bohemian person into a master librarian.

2. **Tags** you apply one or multiple categories to each note. These labels allow for filtering across different kinds of notes. They link information together in categories.

Tags can be used to categorize notes, roles, details, systems, and other hierarchies you may need. For the newbie tagger, start small. Don't create tags you hardly use.

3. **Collections** are groupings of tags, with the help of smart folders. These are meant for larger groups of notes and pull the information for you. Instead of you having to place the note in a certain collection, the tags do it for you. And the beauty is that some notes may belong in multiple collections, without your extra involvement.

4. **Hubs** are curated notes with links to other notes. For instance, my **Family Hub** contains the following index as links to their respective notes:

Copy`• Christmas Wishlist • Christmas Gift Exchange • Christmas Recipes • School Info - Teachers / Calendar / Contact Info • Social Security NumbersInsurance Policies • Family Projects • Doctors (Contact info)`

5. **Journal** is a perpetual calendar system — where one can input things on a daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly basis.

(Once again, Matthias Hilse has provided a swift little shortcut that automates the creation of all these individual notes.)

It takes a bit of tinkering to set up the various internal links. Once done you are set, since each day is intended to be used on that day each year, adding the notes from the current year at the top. The ease of this navigation is well worth the effort.

This is a sweet place to capture thoughts, notes, ideas, and journaling. It works well for plans and goals.

#### Why it works

I find myself spending less time looking for things, and I can use my energy to capture, record, write, and execute as opposed to putting things in the right folder. Or having to remember where I put things.

The entire system even has a minimalistic flair. I find myself looking forward to using it, even after just a few days. It works remarkably well in dark mode.

**Forever Notes** has been an early Christmas gift.

**Implementing this system is like inviting Tony Stark for a free consultation, and setting up Notes so it becomes powerful, bright, and effective for years to come.**
