3 ways to start a speech: „Did you know, that…“ ; Tell a Story (create Awareness of a Problem; Tell the Journey; present the solution); Create a secret and give the answer at the end.

Three effective ways to start a speech include:

1. **Did You Know?**: Begin with a surprising fact or statistic to engage curiosity, such as "Did you know that every year, 18 million hectares of forest are lost?" This grabs attention and sets the stage for your message[3][9].

2. **Tell a Story**: Share a relatable personal story that illustrates a problem, highlights a journey, and leads to a solution. This approach fosters emotional connection and keeps the audience engaged[2][5][4].

3. **Create a Secret**: Start with an intriguing statement or question that hints at a mystery, then reveal the answer at the end of your speech. This technique builds suspense and maintains interest throughout[10].
