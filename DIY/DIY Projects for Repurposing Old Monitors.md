## Overview  
Repurposing old monitors reduces e-waste and unlocks creative DIY opportunities. This guide explores 6 projects, from smart home dashboards to artistic displays, leveraging components like LCD panels, backlights, and Raspberry Pis.  

> [!NOTE]- Key Consideration  
> Always **discharge capacitors** and wear anti-static gear when disassembling monitors. ([iFixit Safety Guide](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+To+Safely+Discharge+a+CRT+Monitor/2143))

## Safety Guidelines  
1. **Disassembly**: Follow [LCD Monitor Teardown Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EXAMPLE).  
2. **Backlight Handling**: CCFL tubes contain mercury; LED strips are safer ([OSHA Guidelines](https://www.osha.gov/)).  
3. **Power Management**: Use 5V buck converters for Raspberry Pi integration ([Adafruit Guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/)).
