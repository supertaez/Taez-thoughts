## Biological Impact  
- **Melatonin Suppression**: 480nm blue light peaks disrupt sleep ([Harvard Study](https://healthysleep.med.harvard.edu/)).  
- **CCT Recommendations**:  
  - Morning: 6500K (alertness)  
  - Evening: 2700K (relaxation)  

---

## DIY Build Guide  
1. **Backlight Mod**: Replace monitor LEDs with tunable strips (e.g., [Philips Hue Lightstrips](https://www.philips-hue.com/)).  
2. **Controller**: ESP8266 + [WLED Firmware](https://kno.wled.ge/).  
3. **Automation**: Sync with [Home Assistant Circadian Component](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/circadian_lighting/).
