# 📚 **The Stricken**  
*By Morgan Shamy*

> *"Magic isn’t a gift—it’s a responsibility. And sometimes, it demands more than you’re willing to give."*  
> — Morgan Shamy

---

## 📝 **Summary / Plot**

*The Stricken* is a captivating young adult fantasy novel that follows the journey of Ember St. Clair, a teenage girl who discovers she has magical abilities after being attacked by shadowy creatures known as the Stricken. Set in a world where magic exists but is feared and misunderstood, Ember learns that her powers are tied to an ancient prophecy—and that she may be the key to saving her realm from destruction.

As Ember navigates this newfound reality, she must confront not only external threats like the Stricken but also internal struggles with self-doubt, identity, and trust. Alongside her loyal friends—Aiden, a skilled warrior, and Lyra, a mysterious healer—Ember embarks on a perilous quest to uncover the truth about her past and fulfill her destiny.

Filled with action-packed battles, heartfelt moments, and unexpected twists, *The Stricken* explores themes of courage, friendship, and the burden of power.

## 🔑 **Key Takeaways & Learnings**

### 1. **Courage Comes from Within**
   - Ember starts as an ordinary girl afraid of her own power but grows into a confident leader through perseverance and self-belief.
   - Lesson: Facing your fears head-on is often the first step toward growth.

### 2. **Friendship Strengthens Resolve**
   - The bond between Ember, Aiden, and Lyra proves essential in overcoming challenges.
   - Quote: *"Alone we are strong, but together we are unstoppable."*

### 3. **Power Requires Responsibility**
   - Magic in the story symbolizes any form of influence or talent—it must be used wisely and ethically.
   - Application: Whether at school, work, or home, using your skills for good can make a lasting impact.

### 4. **Identity is Fluid**
   - Ember grapples with reconciling her old life with her new responsibilities, highlighting the universal struggle of finding one’s place in the world.

### 5. **Hope Prevails Over Despair**
   - Despite setbacks, the characters maintain hope, which fuels their determination to fight for a better future.
