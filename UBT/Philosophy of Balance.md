# Taoist Yin-Yang and UBT

## Core Concepts
- **Yin**: Passive, receptive energy.
- **Yang**: Active, assertive energy.
- **Interdependence**: Each contains the seed of its opposite.

## UBT Connections
- **Dynamic Equilibrium**: Direct parallel to UBT’s fluid balance (e.g., market boom/bust cycles).
- **Non-Static Harmony**: Both reject rigid stability.

## Key Differences
- **Scope**: Taoism is metaphysical; UBT applies to tangible systems.
