## The Energy Transition Acceleration — The Unstoppable Force

After February 28, 2026, the energy transition is a **national security imperative** for every energy-importing nation on earth.

The GCC states have known this transition was coming. The war has compressed those decades into years.

- Choosing to stand on principle rather than submit

The effectiveness of this strategy extends far beyond the Muslim world. The Karbala narrative — the righteous underdog destroyed by overwhelming imperial force — is universally legible.
