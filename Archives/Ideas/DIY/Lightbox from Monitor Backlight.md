## Overview  
Transform a non-functional monitor’s backlight into a **high-CRI light source** for photography, art, or circadian lighting.  

---

## Step-by-Step Guide  
1. **Disassemble Monitor**: Remove LCD layer ([Video Guide](https://youtu.be/EXAMPLE1)).  
2. **Reframe Backlight**: Mount in IKEA RIBBA frame ([Mod Instructions](https://www.instructables.com/)).  
3. **Power Setup**:  
   - CCFL: Use original inverter (caution: high voltage).  
   - LED: Direct-wire to 12V PSU ([Circuit Diagram](https://imgur.com/EXAMPLE)).  

---

## Technical Specs  
| Parameter         | CCFL Backlight | LED Backlight |  
|--------------------|----------------|---------------|  
| Luminosity         | 3000 lm        | 2500 lm       |  
| Color Temperature  | 6500K          | Adjustable     |  
| Power Draw         | 40W            | 18W           |  

> [!WARNING]- CCFL Safety  
> Contains mercury vapor; dispose via [Call2Recycle](https://www.call2recycle.org/).

---

## Applications  
- **Photography**: Diffused lighting for product shots ([Example Setup](https://flic.kr/p/EXAMPLE)).  
- **Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD)**: 10,000 lux at 12" distance ([NIH Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/EXAMPLE/)).
